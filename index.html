Tips for a good hologram
  • Line it up first: in Test pattern, rotate and mirror until “TOP” is upright and “HOLO” reads normally in the reflection. These settings are saved.
  • Filming: front camera, dark wall or room behind you, a lamp or a phone torch lighting your face from the front. Wear something light-coloured, because dark clothes disappear.
  • Background cut: raise it until the background is fully black; lower it if your face starts losing pieces. Shrink the face oval to crop away anything that's left.
  • Black is invisible in the reflection, so anything that isn't pure black will glow.
  • Sending: send her the raw video on WhatsApp. She opens this page, taps Video and picks it, and her phone does the processing.
